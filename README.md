## About me

I am a civil engineer moving into **data analytics and automation**. My engineering background in hydraulic modelling and risk assessment gave me a habit of working carefully with data. Now I focus on turning raw data into clear, verifiable insights and on automating repetitive data work.

What I care about:

- **Data quality**: cleaning, validation and documenting where data comes from.
- **Analysis and reporting**: SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau and Excel.
- **Automation**: APIs, workflow tools and AI-assisted workflows, always with human review.
